The man behind the trophy

Fr. Schlooz

The Ever Rolling Trophy carries the name of Fr. Schlooz, a Salesian priest remembered by the Don Bosco Youth Centre and the people of Varadarajanpet. Every December the tournament brings the young players he cared for back onto the pitch.

The champions take the trophy home for a year and bring it back to defend it. The crest on this page is the new DBYCFA badge: a floodlit ball under the Don Bosco name, laurels for the winners and three stars for the youth centre, the academy and the town.
